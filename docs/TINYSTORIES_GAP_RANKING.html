Local recognition of those floating forms is not recognition of the W8A8 computation.

Library reuse and FPGA consequences

Lean core Int/BitVec and tensor indexing supply the signed arithmetic and traversal ingredients. FloatLib supplies existing floating arithmetic, rounding and storage words. Mathlib or CSLib may help later specifications and proofs; they are not substitutes for missing executable PyTorch operators. Implementing integer dot products and preserving quantization boundaries are necessary for the intended W8A8 FPGA datapath. Floating attention, normalization and rescaling remain part of this target. Operator counts alone do not establish speed, area, timing closure or language quality.
